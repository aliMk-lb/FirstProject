# Main Heading & Points

People trust brands that look consistent. When your website design changes from page to page, it feels unprofessional.

## What “branding consistency” includes
- Colors and typography
- Button styles and spacing
- Tone of writing
- Image style

## Why it matters
- Stronger recognition
- Higher trust
- Better conversion

## Final Thoughts
Branding isn’t only a logo — it’s the whole experience your website delivers.
