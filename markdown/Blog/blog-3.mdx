# Main Heading & Points

Ads stop the moment you stop paying. Organic growth keeps working long-term through search visibility and content.

## What organic growth means
- SEO (search rankings)
- Content marketing (blogs, guides)
- Brand trust (authority)

## SEO basics that matter most
- Clear page titles + meta descriptions
- Fast load speed and mobile-first design
- Strong headings (H1/H2) and internal links

## Content that actually ranks
- “How-to” guides
- Problem/solution articles
- Comparison posts
- FAQs your customers search

## Final Thoughts
Organic growth is slower at first — but it becomes the most cost-effective marketing channel over time.
