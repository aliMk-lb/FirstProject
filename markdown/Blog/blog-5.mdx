# Main Heading & Points

A website can lose customers even when it looks modern. The issue is often hidden in the user journey.

## Top reasons you’re losing customers
- Slow loading pages
- Confusing navigation
- No clear value proposition
- Weak or hidden call-to-action
- Lack of trust signals

## Fix the journey
- Explain what you do in one sentence
- Put CTA above the fold
- Use testimonials or client logos
- Make contact easy (form + WhatsApp)

## Final Thoughts
If your website doesn’t guide users, they won’t guess. Clarity is what converts.
