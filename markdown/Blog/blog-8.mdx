# Main Heading & Points

Small design problems can destroy conversions. Most visitors won’t complain — they’ll just leave.

## Mistakes to avoid
1. No clear headline/value
2. Too many fonts/colors
3. Weak CTA
4. Bad spacing and clutter
5. Stocky-looking images everywhere
6. Poor mobile design
7. Slow loading pages
8. Long forms
9. No trust signals
10. Inconsistent branding

## Final Thoughts
Good design makes users feel confident. Fix the basics and your website instantly improves.
