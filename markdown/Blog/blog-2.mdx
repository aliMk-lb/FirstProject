# Main Heading & Points

Speed is the first impression your website makes. If it’s slow, visitors assume the business is outdated or unreliable — and they leave.

## What slow websites cause
- Higher bounce rates
- Lower Google rankings
- Fewer leads and sales

## What actually makes websites slow
- Large images and videos
- Too many scripts/plugins
- No caching/minification
- Heavy animations

## Quick speed wins
- Convert images to WebP and compress them
- Enable caching + Gzip/Brotli
- Minify CSS/JS
- Lazy-load images below the fold

## Final Thoughts
A fast site feels premium. If your website is slow, you’re paying for traffic you can’t keep.
